Privacy policy

Last updated: October 18, 2026

MotiPad lets your iPhone control a Windows computer. The app and receiver contain no advertising, usage analytics, or automatic diagnostic uploads. Pairing and control data are handled on your devices and local network.

1. Scope and contact

This policy covers the MotiPad iOS app, Windows receiver, and product website. ‘We’ means the MotiPad developer. Send privacy questions or data requests to the email address below.

2. Device communication and local data

To discover, pair, authenticate, and control computers, devices exchange device names, identifiers, local IP addresses, connection status, and touch control commands over the local network. Pairing records, authentication keys, and preferences are stored locally and are not automatically uploaded to developer servers. The Windows receiver may save a local log beside its executable, containing device names, phone identifiers, IP addresses, and connection events. Logs are not automatically uploaded.

3. Local network permission

The iOS app uses local network permission to discover and connect to computers on the same local network. This does not grant GPS location access. You can change permission in iOS Settings; denying it may prevent discovery and connection.

4. Advertising, tracking, and diagnostics

MotiPad contains no advertising, third-party usage analytics, or automatic crash-reporting SDKs. It does not use advertising identifiers or track you across apps or websites. The release iOS app does not include development touch recording, replay, or latency logging. Windows local logs are not automatically sent to us.

5. App Store purchases

Purchases are handled through Apple StoreKit. Apple processes payment information; MotiPad does not provide us with your card number or bank account details. The app uses transaction and purchase entitlement information supplied by Apple to determine available features. Apple’s own processing is governed by its privacy policy and terms.

Apple Privacy Policy

6. Contacting us and retention

If you email us or voluntarily share screenshots or logs, we receive your email address, message, and attachments solely to reply, troubleshoot, and handle your request. Email providers process messages under their own terms. Avoid sharing unnecessary personal information, passwords, or payment details. We retain support correspondence only as needed to resolve issues and for reasonable follow-up. You may request access or deletion by email; if retention is legally required, we will explain the applicable circumstances.

7. This website

This website adds no advertising, analytics tools, or nonessential cookies. To deliver pages, maintain security, and prevent abuse, hosting services may process IP addresses, browser information, and request times, and use necessary access controls. This is separate from local app data processing. External websites apply their own privacy policies.

8. Your choices and data security

You can remove paired devices, change local network permission, and manage or delete Windows local logs. Removing a pairing does not necessarily delete earlier logs, and uninstalling may not remove all system-stored data such as keychain entries. Authentication keys use the system keychain on iOS and system data protection on Windows. Keep your devices secure and use trusted networks. We do not sell personal data or provide local app data to advertisers.

9. Updates

We will update this policy and its date when our data practices change, and provide information or obtain permission for new data uses where required.
